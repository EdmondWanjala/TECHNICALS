Electromagnetism is the fundamental physical force that describes how electric charges and magnetic fields interact with each other.

    Physics Overview
* Electric Charges & Fields: Stationary or moving charges create electric fields (E) that exert forces on other charged particles, quantified by Coulomb's Law.
* Magnetic Fields & Forces: Moving electric charges (currents) generate magnetic fields (B), which in turn exert forces on other moving charges via the Lorentz force.
* Electromagnetic Induction: A changing magnetic field creates an electric field (Faraday's Law), and a changing electric field creates a magnetic field (Ampère-Maxwell Law).
* Maxwell's Equations: Four fundamental mathematical equations unify electricity and magnetism and predict propagating electromagnetic waves, such as light and radio waves.

    Key Topics
* Electrostatics: The study of stationary electric charges, electric potentials, and capacitance.
* Magnetostatics: The study of steady electric currents and constant magnetic fields.
* Electrodynamics: The study of time-varying fields, radiation, and energy propagation.
* Circuit Theory Components: Resistance, inductance, and capacitance, which arise directly from electromagnetic field properties.

    Applications in Electronics and Computer Engineering
* Transistors and Semiconductors: The building blocks of microprocessors and modern computing rely on electric field control to switch and amplify current.
* Signal Integrity and PCB Design: High-speed computer chips and circuit boards manage parasitic inductance and capacitance to prevent electromagnetic interference (EMI).
* Data Storage: Hard disk drives use tiny magnetic fields to read and write binary data (0s and 1s) on platters.
* Power Delivery and Transformers: Power supplies and voltage regulators use inductors and transformers governed by induction principles to convert and filter clean power for computer hardware.
* Wireless Communication: Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and cellular data rely on antennas emitting oscillating electromagnetic waves to transmit digital packets.
