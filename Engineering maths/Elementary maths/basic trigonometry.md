        BASIC TRIGONOMETRY AND MATHEMATICAL RELATIONSHIP
Trigonometry studies relationships between angles and sides of triangles.
For a right-angled triangle:
    SOH-CAH-TOA
    sin θ = Opposite/Hypotenuse
    cos θ = Adjacent/Hypotenuse
    tan θ = Opposite/Adjacent
Therefore:
    tan θ = sin θ/cos θ.

RECIPROCAL TRIGONOMETRIC FUNCTIONS;
cosec θ = 1/sin θ
sec θ = 1/cos θ
cot θ = 1/tan θ = cos θ/sin θ.

PYTHAGOREAN IDENTITIES;
sin²θ + cos²θ = 1
1 + tan²θ = sec²θ
1 + cot²θ = cosec²θ.

COMMON ANGLE VALUES;
Angle:       0°    30°    45°    60°    90°
sin θ:       0     1/2    √2/2   √3/2   1
cos θ:       1     √3/2   √2/2   1/2    0
tan θ:       0     √3/3   1      √3     Undefined

ANGLE MEASUREMENT;
Degrees and radians are two ways of measuring angles.
    180° = π radians.
To convert degrees to radians:
    θ(rad) = θ(degrees) × π/180.
To convert radians to degrees:
    θ(degrees) = θ(rad) × 180/π.

SINE AND COSINE RULES;
Sine rule:
    a/sin A = b/sin B = c/sin C.
Cosine rule:
    c² = a² + b² - 2ab cos C.
Where a, b and c are opposite angles A, B and C respectively.
Area of any triangle:
    A = ½ab sin C.

BASIC TRIGONOMETRIC GRAPH;
y = sin x
y = cos x
y = tan x.
For sine and cosine:
• Amplitude = 1.
• Period = 2π radians or 360°.
For tangent:
• Period = π radians or 180°.
• Vertical asymptotes occur at x = π/2 + nπ.
For a general sinusoidal function:
    y = A sin(ωx + φ) + C
Where:
A = Amplitude (|A|).
ω = Angular frequency.
φ = Phase angle.
C = Vertical displacement.
Period:
    T = 2π/|ω|.

APPLICATIONS OF TRIGONOMETRY;
• Resolving forces into horizontal and vertical components.
• Calculating displacement and velocity components.
• Analysing AC electrical waveforms.
• Determining angles and distances.
• Mechanical and structural design.
• Robotics and navigation.

ENGINEERING TIP:
Understand both degree and radian modes on your calculator. Many engineering equations, especially calculus and signal-processing equations, require angles in radians.
