                GEOMETRY AND MENSURATION
  BASIC GEOMETRIC CONCEPTS;
Point: Exact position in space.
Line: Straight path extending indefinitely.
Line segment: Part of a line between two endpoints.
Ray: Line with one endpoint extending infinitely in one direction.
Angle: Measure of rotation between two intersecting lines or rays.
 Types of angles:
Acute: Less than 90°.
Right: Exactly 90°.
Obtuse: Between 90° and 180°.
Straight: 180°.
Reflex: Between 180° and 360°.
Full rotation: 360°.

  ANGLE RELATIONSHIPS;
Complementary angles:
    Sum = 90°.
Supplementary angles:
    Sum = 180°.
Angles around a point:
    Sum = 360°.
Angles on a straight line:
    Sum = 180°.
Vertically opposite angles are equal.
 For parallel lines cut by a transversal:
• Corresponding angles are equal.
• Alternate interior angles are equal.
• Co-interior angles sum to 180°.

  TRIANGLES;
Sum of interior angles = 180°.
Types:
• Equilateral: Three equal sides and three 60° angles.
• Isosceles: Two equal sides and two equal base angles.
• Scalene: All sides and angles generally unequal.
• Right-angled: One angle is 90°.
Area:
    A = ½bh.
Perimeter:
    P = a + b + c.
Pythagoras' theorem:
    c² = a² + b².
Where c is the hypotenuse of a right-angled triangle.

  QUADRILATERALS;
Square:
    Area = a²
    Perimeter = 4a.
Rectangle:
    Area = lw
    Perimeter = 2(l+w).
Parallelogram:
    Area = bh.
Trapezium:
    Area = ½(a+b)h.
Rhombus:
    Area = ½d₁d₂.

CIRCLES;
Circumference:
    C = 2πr = πd.
Area:
    A = πr².
Arc length:
    s = rθ.
Sector area:
    A = ½r²θ.
The last two formulas require θ in radians.

THREE-DIMENSIONAL GEOMETRY AND MENSURATION;
Cube:
    Volume = a³
    Surface area = 6a².
Cuboid:
    Volume = lwh
    Surface area = 2(lw + lh + wh).
Cylinder:
    Volume = πr²h
    Curved surface area = 2πrh
    Total surface area = 2πr(h+r).
Cone:
    Volume = ⅓πr²h
    Curved surface area = πrl
    Total surface area = πr(l+r).
Sphere:
    Volume = ⁴⁄₃πr³
    Surface area = 4πr².
Pyramid:
    Volume = ⅓ × Base area × Height.
Prism:
    Volume = Base area × Length.

COORDINATE GEOMETRY;
A point in a Cartesian coordinate system is represented by (x,y).
Distance between two points:
    d = √[(x₂-x₁)² + (y₂-y₁)²]
Midpoint:
    M = ((x₁+x₂)/2, (y₁+y₂)/2)
Gradient/slope:
    m = (y₂-y₁)/(x₂-x₁)
Equation of a straight line:
    y = mx + c.
Where m is the gradient and c is the y-intercept.
