        RATIOS, PROPORTIONS, PERCENTAGES AND VARIATION
RATIOS;
A ratio compares two or more quantities of the same kind.
    a:b = a/b
Example:
If two resistors have values of 10 Ω and 20 Ω, their ratio is:
    10:20 = 1:2.
A ratio can be simplified by dividing all terms by their common factor.

PROPORTIONS;
A proportion states that two ratios are equal.
    a/b = c/d
Cross multiplication:
    ad = bc
Example:
If 2/3 = x/12:
    3x = 24
    x = 8.
  DIRECT PROPORTION;
Two quantities are directly proportional if an increase or decrease in one produces a corresponding increase or decrease in the other.
    y ∝ x
Therefore:
    y = kx
Where k is the constant of proportionality.
Example:
Distance travelled at constant speed is directly proportional to time.
    d = vt.
  INVERSE PROPORTION;
Two quantities are inversely proportional if an increase in one produces a decrease in the other.
    y ∝ 1/x
Therefore:
    y = k/x
Or:
    xy = k.
Example:
For a fixed distance, travel time is inversely proportional to speed.

PERCENTAGES;
Percentage means a quantity expressed per hundred.
    Percentage = (Part/Whole) × 100%
Finding a percentage of a quantity:
    Value = (Percentage/100) × Total.
Percentage change:
    % Change = [(New value - Original value)/Original value] × 100%
Positive result = Percentage increase.
Negative result = Percentage decrease.

RATES;
A rate compares two quantities with different units.
Examples:
• Speed = Distance/Time.
• Acceleration = Change in velocity/Time.
• Power = Energy/Time.
• Current = Charge/Time.
• Flow rate = Volume/Time.

SCALE AND PROPORTIONAL REASONING;
A scale represents the relationship between a drawing or model and its actual size.
    Scale = Drawing dimension/Actual dimension.
Example:
A scale of 1:100 means one unit on the drawing represents 100 corresponding units in reality.
 Applications:
Engineering drawings, maps, models and technical design.

ENGINEERING TIP:
Always ensure that quantities being compared are expressed in compatible units. Distinguish carefully between direct and inverse proportionality.
