Elementary mathematics provides the foundational language, number sense, and logical framework used to understand quantities, structures, space, and change.

Overview;
Elementary mathematics covers primary arithmetic through introductory secondary algebraic and geometric concepts. It focuses on building number sense, mastering computational accuracy, developing spatial reasoning, and applying logic to everyday problem-solving.

Key Topics;
• Number Sense & Numeration: Whole numbers, place value (base-10 system), integers, fractions, decimals, and percentages.
• Basic Operations: Addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division, along with mental math, estimation, and number theory (prime numbers, factors, GCF, and LCM).
• Algebraic Thinking: Simple variables, expressions, and solving one-step and two-step equations, alongside number patterns and sequences.
• Geometry: Identifying 2D and 3D shapes, calculating perimeter, area, and volume, and understanding properties like symmetry and congruence.
• Measurement: Quantifying length, mass, volume, time, and money using standard metric or imperial units.
• Data, Statistics & Probability: Collecting data, reading bar or line graphs, calculating measures of central tendency (mean, median, mode, range), and basic probability.

Real-World Applications;
• Daily Financial Management: Budgeting, calculating grocery costs, making change, handling interest, and understanding discounts or taxes.
• Cooking and Scaling: Measuring ingredients, doubling or halving recipes, and working with fractional proportions.
• Construction and Design: Measuring spaces, computing area and perimeter for flooring or fencing, and reading scale maps.
• Time and Scheduling: Managing daily itineraries, travel estimates, and rate-time-distance calculations.
• Data Interpretation: Reading weather charts, analyzing sports statistics, and understanding news graphics or surveys.
