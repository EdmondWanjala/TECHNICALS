PCB (Printed Circuit Board) design is the engineering process of turning an abstract electrical schematic into a physical, functional board layout that holds and connects electronic components.

Overview
• Definition: Translates a logical circuit diagram (schematic) and Bill of Materials (BOM) into a spatial arrangement of copper tracks, pads, and insulating layers.
• Core Foundation: Uses a fiberglass-epoxy substrate (commonly FR4) layered with conductive copper sheets.
• Workflow: Concept/specifications → schematic capture → component placement → trace routing → Design Rule Check (DRC) / Design for Manufacturing (DFM) verification.

Key Topics
• PCB Stack-Up & Layers: Organizing single-sided, double-sided, or multi-layer boards (4 to 32+ layers) with dedicated internal ground and power planes to control impedance and shield signals.
• Signal Integrity & Impedance Control: Managing high-speed traces (such as USB, Ethernet, or HDMI) to prevent signal reflection, skew, and crosstalk.
• Thermal Management: Distributing heat-generating components, using thermal vias, and applying copper pours to prevent localized overheating or board delamination.
• Design Rules & Constraints (DFM/DFA): Enforcing clearance, creepage, trace-width, and annular-ring standards (often guided by IPC standards like IPC-2221).
• EDA Software: Utilizing computer-aided design tools like KiCad, Altium, or Eagle for schematic entry and layout.

Applications
• Consumer Electronics: Smartphones, smartwatches, and fitness trackers utilizing high-density and flexible PCBs.
• Automotive & Aerospace: Engine control units, radar, navigation systems, and satellite hardware requiring high-temperature and vibration-resistant substrates.
• Telecommunications: 5G base stations, routers, and high-speed switches needing tight RF and microwave impedance matching.
• Medical Devices: Wearable health monitors, diagnostic equipment, and compact internal/external implants.
• Industrial Automation & IoT: Smart sensors, robotic controllers, and renewable energy power converters.
