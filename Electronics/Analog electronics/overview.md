Analog electronics is a branch of electronics that deals with electrical signals that vary continuously and smoothly over time, mirroring real-world physical quantities like sound, light, temperature, and pressure.

Overview
• Continuous Signals: Unlike digital systems that use discrete binary states (0s and 1s), analog systems process uninterrupted waves of voltage or current.
• Core Components: Built using passive devices (resistors, capacitors, inductors) and active devices (diodes, bipolar junction transistors, field-effect transistors, and operational amplifiers).
• Interface Layer: Acts as the real-world front end that conditions, amplifies, or filters physical measurements before they are processed or converted by digital systems.

Key Topics
• Semiconductor Physics & Diodes: Study of p-n junctions, rectifiers, Zener regulators, and wave-shaping diode circuits.
• Transistors (BJTs & FETs): Bipolar Junction Transistors (BJTs) and Field-Effect Transistors (MOSFETs, JFETs) configured for biasing, switching, and signal amplification in the active region.
• Operational Amplifiers (Op-Amps): High-gain differential amplifiers used in feedback loops for math operations, buffering, and signal conditioning.
• Frequency Response & Filtering: Low-pass, high-pass, band-pass, and band-stop passive or active filters that isolate or attenuate specific frequencies.
• Oscillators & Waveform Generators: Circuits (such as LC or RC oscillators) that produce continuous periodic signals like sine, square, or triangle waves.
• Power Regulation & Amplification: Linear and switching voltage regulators, AC-to-DC converters, and power amplifiers driving heavy loads.
• Analog-to-Digital and Digital-to-Analog Conversion (ADCs/DACs): The bridge translating continuous real-world waveforms into digital data and back.

Key Applications
• Audio Systems: Preamplifiers, mixers, equalizers, and power amplifiers in home stereos, guitars, and microphones.
• Radio Frequency (RF) & Communications: Tuners, mixers, modulators, and demodulators for wireless, television, and satellite transmission.
• Power Supplies & Management: AC-to-DC rectification, battery chargers, and feedback-controlled voltage regulators.
• Medical Instrumentation: Signal acquisition and filtering for electrocardiographs (ECGs), pacemakers, and patient monitors.
• Industrial & Automotive Control: Sensor interfaces (thermocouples, strain gauges, pressure transducers), engine control units (ECUs), and motor drive feedback loops.
