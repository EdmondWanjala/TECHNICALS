Power electronics is the branch of electrical engineering that uses solid-state semiconductor devices to control, condition, and convert electric power from one form to another with high efficiency and minimal energy loss.

Overview
• Core Purpose: Unlike signal electronics (which process information), power electronics processes substantial electrical energy—ranging from watts to gigawatts—by rapidly switching semiconductors on and off rather than wasting excess energy as heat.
• Primary Functions:
	• Conversion: Changing voltage, current, or frequency (AC to DC, DC to DC, DC to AC, and AC to AC).
	• Control: Regulating power flow to match exact load requirements.
	• Conditioning: Smoothing out voltage and current waveforms for stability.
• Advanced Materials: Traditional silicon devices are increasingly complemented or replaced by wide-bandgap semiconductors (such as Silicon Carbide - SiC and Gallium Nitride - GaN), which allow higher operating temperatures, faster switching speeds, and efficiencies exceeding 98–99%.

Key Topics & Components
Power Semiconductor Devices (Switches);
• Diodes: Uncontrolled devices for rectification and freewheeling (standard, fast-recovery, Schottky).
• Thyristors (SCRs, Triacs, GTOs): Controlled turn-on (and sometimes turn-off) devices used in high-power phase control and line-commutated systems.
• Power Transistors (BJTs, MOSFETs, IGBTs): Fully controlled high-frequency switches; MOSFETs dominate low-to-medium voltage high-frequency uses, while IGBTs dominate medium-to-high voltage and current applications.
Power Converters (Topologies);
• Rectifiers (AC to DC): Convert alternating current to direct current (e.g., single-phase and three-phase controlled or uncontrolled bridges).
• Choppers / DC-DC Converters (DC to DC): Step up or step down DC voltages (buck, boost, buck-boost converters).
• Inverters (DC to AC): Convert direct current into alternating current (e.g., voltage source inverters used in solar and motor drives).
• AC Voltage Controllers / Cycloconverters (AC to AC): Change AC voltage levels or frequency directly.
Support & Control Elements;
• Passive Components: Inductors, capacitors, and transformers for energy storage, filtering ripples, and managing impedance.
• Control & Gating Circuits: Microprocessors, digital signal processors (DSPs), and FPGAs running algorithms to manage switching timing and feedback loops.
• Protection Mechanisms: Snubber circuits, heat sinks, and overvoltage/overcurrent fuses to prevent thermal or electrical failure.

Applications
• Consumer & Domestic Electronics: Laptop chargers, smartphone power adapters, televisions, refrigerators, microwave ovens, and induction heating.
• Transportation & Electric Vehicles (EVs): Traction motor drives, battery chargers, regenerative braking systems, and high-speed rail.
• Renewable Energy & Smart Grids: Solar PV inverters, wind turbine frequency converters, High-Voltage Direct Current (HVDC) transmission links, and Uninterruptible Power Supplies (UPS).
• Industrial & Aerospace Systems: Variable-speed motor drives, robotics, heavy-duty pump controllers, elevators, and aerospace power distribution.
