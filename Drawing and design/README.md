                        DRAWING AND DESIGN

This folder contains independent study of engineering drawing,
CAD, modelling, visualization, and technical design.
Engineering drawing and design is the language of engineering, used to communicate how structures and products are built. 
It translates conceptual ideas into precise, standardized visual blueprints for manufacturing and construction.
    
    Key Topics
* Technical Lettering & Line Types: Standardized fonts and line styles (e.g., hidden, center, dimension lines) that convey specific geometric meanings.
* Orthographic Projection: Standard multi-view drawings (front, top, and side views) that represent 3D objects accurately on 2D paper.
* Isometric & Axonometric Projection: 3D pictorial representations that show all three dimensions simultaneously for quick visualization.
* Dimensioning & Tolerancing (GD&T): The system of specifying exact sizes and the allowable variations (tolerances) for manufacturing accuracy.
* Computer-Aided Design (CAD): Utilizing software like AutoCAD, SolidWorks, or Autodesk Inventor to create digital 2D drafts and 3D models.
* Sectional Views: "Cutting open" an object in a drawing to reveal its internal components and complex features.
    
    Real-World Applications
* Aerospace & Automotive: Mapping out engine components, chassis designs, and aerodynamic body panels to strict safety margins.
* Architecture & Civil Engineering: Creating structural blueprints for bridges, skyscrapers, and piping layouts.
* Manufacturing & Robotics: Designing gears, molds, and robotic arms with precise tolerances for automated assembly lines.
