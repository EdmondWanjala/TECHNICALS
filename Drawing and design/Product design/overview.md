Product design is the strategic, human-centered process of identifying a user problem and creating a functional, marketable physical or digital solution. It bridges the gap between what users need and what a business can build.

Overview;
Unlike single-discipline roles (such as graphic or UI design), product design looks at the big picture. It merges user experience (UX), user interface (UI), engineering constraints, and business strategy from concept all the way to launch and iteration.
• Core Goal: Make products useful, usable, desirable, and feasible to manufacture or code.
• The Mindset: Iterative problem-solving rather than just making things look pretty.

Key Topics & Principles;
• User-Centered Design (UCD): Putting real human pain points, interviews, and empathy mapping at the center of every decision.
• Functionality & Usability: Ensuring the product works reliably, flows logically, and removes friction from the user journey.
• Aesthetics & Branding: Crafting visual appeal, typography, color systems, and physical form factors that build an emotional connection.
• Manufacturability & Technical Feasibility: Designing with realistic production costs, material science, CAD specs, or software architecture in mind.
• Business Strategy & ROI: Balancing user delight with company revenue goals, market fit, and scalable product roadmaps.
• The Design Process (Double Diamond / Iterative Cycle): Moving through research, ideation, wireframing, prototyping, user testing, and post-launch refinement.

Applications;
Product design spans multiple industries and form factors:
• Digital Products: Mobile apps, SaaS platforms, enterprise software, and websites (focusing on UX/UI, information architecture, and microcopy).
• Industrial & Physical Products: Consumer electronics, furniture, household appliances, and automotive parts (focusing on industrial design and CAD).
• Formulated & Molecular Goods: Skincare, cosmetics, pharmaceuticals, and engineered chemical blends (combining lab experimentation with functional attributes).
• Service & System Design: Structuring end-to-end customer experiences, workflows, and physical-digital touchpoints
