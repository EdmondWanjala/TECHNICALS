3D modeling is the process of creating a three-dimensional digital representation of an object, surface, or environment using specialized software.

Key Topics & Core Concepts;
• Basic Building Blocks: Models are built from vertices (points in 3D space), edges (lines connecting vertices), and polygons/faces (surfaces enclosed by edges).
• Coordinate Space: Objects are positioned using global space (world axes: X, Y, Z) and local space (an object's own pivot point and orientation).
• Core Modeling Actions: Most workflows rely on fundamental operations like extruding (pulling out geometry), beveling (rounding edges), inset, merging, and using the knife tool.
• Modeling Techniques:
	• Polygonal Modeling: Direct mesh manipulation using triangles and quads; ideal for games and animation.
	• Digital Sculpting: Shaping a mesh like a block of digital clay; great for organic characters and creatures.
	• Solid & Parametric/CAD Modeling: Building volumetric objects using exact mathematical measurements and 2D sketches; used in engineering.
	• NURBS Modeling: Using smooth mathematical curves to craft precise, fluid industrial surfaces (e.g., automotive design).
• Workflow Stages: Projects generally follow a structured path: blockout (primary shapes) → secondary forms (features) → tertiary details (fine lines, textures, and polish).

Popular Software Tools;
• Beginner-Friendly: TinkerCAD and SketchUp
• All-Round / Creative & Animation: Blender, Autodesk Maya, 3ds Max, and Cinema 4D
• Digital Sculpting: ZBrush
• Engineering & CAD: Fusion 360, SolidWorks, and Rhino3D

Key Applications;
• Entertainment & Media: Designing characters, props, vehicles, and visual effects (VFX) for video games and films.
• Architecture & Interior Design: Crafting building structures, spatial floor plans, and client walkthroughs.
• Product Design & Manufacturing: Rapid prototyping, functional testing, and prepping parts for CNC machining or 3D printing.
• Aerospace & Automotive: Stress-testing vehicle components and engineering aerodynamic parts.
• Medical & Science: Creating anatomical models, surgical planning tools, and prosthetic designs.
