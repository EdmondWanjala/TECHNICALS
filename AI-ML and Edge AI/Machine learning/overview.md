Machine learning (ML) is a subset of Artificial Intelligence (AI) that enables computer systems to learn, recognize patterns, and make decisions from data without being explicitly programmed for every scenario. 
Instead of hard-coding rules, algorithms build mathematical models from training data to generalize and predict outcomes on new, unseen data.

Key Topics & Learning Paradigms:
• Supervised Learning: Trains models on labeled datasets (input-output pairs) to map or predict future values.
	• Regression: Predicts continuous numeric values (e.g., house prices, temperature).
	• Classification: Assigns data to discrete categories (e.g., spam vs. non-spam emails, disease diagnosis).
• Unsupervised Learning: Analyzes unlabeled data to discover hidden structures, groupings, or anomalies.
	• Clustering: Groups similar items together (e.g., K-means for customer segmentation).
	• Dimensionality Reduction & Association: Simplifies complex data while retaining vital features (e.g., PCA, market basket analysis).
• Semi-Supervised Learning: Uses a small amount of labeled data combined with a large pool of unlabeled data to improve training efficiency.
• Reinforcement Learning: Trains an agent to make a sequence of decisions in an environment by maximizing a reward/penalty feedback loop (e.g., robotics, game playing).
• Deep Learning: Utilizes multi-layered Artificial Neural Networks (including CNNs for vision and Transformers for language) to model complex patterns in large datasets.
• Workflow & Operations (MLOps): Involves data collection, preprocessing, feature engineering, model training, cross-validation, and deployment using tools like Python, PyTorch, and TensorFlow.

Real-World Applications:
• Healthcare: Disease detection from medical scans, predictive analytics for patient outcomes, and accelerated drug discovery.
• Finance: Fraudulent transaction detection, credit/loan risk assessment, and algorithmic trading.
• E-Commerce & Entertainment: Personalized product recommendations, dynamic pricing, and customer demand forecasting.
• Autonomous Systems & Transportation: Computer vision for object recognition in self-driving cars, route optimization, and traffic prediction.
• Natural Language Processing (NLP): Virtual assistants (like Siri or Alexa), automatic language translation, and generative AI content tools.
• Cybersecurity & IT: Email spam filtering, anomaly detection, and network intrusion prevention.
