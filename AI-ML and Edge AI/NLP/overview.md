Natural Language Processing (NLP) is a branch of artificial intelligence that lets computers read, interpret, understand, and generate human language. 
It bridges the gap between human communication and computer data through computational linguistics, statistics, and deep learning.

Key Topics & Concepts;
• Syntax and Semantics:
	• Syntax looks at sentence structure and grammar rules (such as parts of speech).
	• Semantics looks at the actual meaning behind words and how they relate to one another.
• Text Preprocessing:
	• Cleaning raw text through tokenization (breaking text into words or pieces), lowercasing, removing stop words, and lemmatization (reducing words to their root form).
• Text Representation:
	• Transforming words into data models using Bag-of-Words (BoW), TF-IDF, or modern word embeddings and transformer-based contextual vectors.
• Core Techniques:
	• Named Entity Recognition (NER), Part-of-Speech (POS) tagging, sentiment analysis, and text summarization.
You can read more about core building blocks on the GeeksforGeeks Introduction to NLP guide.

Key Applications;
• Conversational AI & Chatbots:
	• Virtual assistants like Alexa, Google Assistant, and customer support bots process spoken or written text to answer questions.
• Machine Translation:
	• Automatically converting text or speech from one language to another with high accuracy.
• Sentiment Analysis:
	• Scanning social media, reviews, or survey feedback to detect positive, negative, or neutral public emotion.
• Text Classification & Spam Filtering:
	• Automatically sorting emails, flagging junk mail, or labeling document topics.
• Grammar & Predictive Text:
	• Powering autocorrect, smart compose, and writing tools like Grammarly.
