                SIGNALS, SYSTEMS AND COMMUNICATION

This folder contains independent study of signals, systems, control,
signal processing, and communication technologies.
Signals, systems, and communication form the foundational framework used to represent, process, and transmit information across modern electronic and mechanical technologies.
    
    Overview
* Signals: Physical quantities or functions that vary with time, space, or another independent variable to convey information. Examples include human voice, radio waves, and electrical voltages.
* Systems: Physical devices, circuits, or software algorithms that accept an input signal (excitation) and transform it into an output signal (response).
* Communication: The process of encoding, transmitting via wired or wireless channels, and decoding data between a sender and a receiver.
    
    Key Topics
* Signal Classifications: Division into continuous-time vs. discrete-time, and analog vs. digital formats.
* System Properties: Core attributes including linearity, time-invariance (LTI systems), causality, and stability.
* Transform Analysis: Mathematical tools like the Fourier series, Fourier transform, Laplace transform, and Z-transform used to shift signals from the time domain to the frequency domain for analysis.
* Modulation & Demodulation: Shifting signal frequencies via amplitude, frequency, or phase modulation so they can travel across long distances through antennas.
* Filtering & Noise Management: Removing unwanted environmental disturbance or noise using low-pass, high-pass, or digital filters.
    
    Real-World Applications
* Wireless Telecommunications: 4G, 5G broadband, and Wi-Fi networks rely on signal modulation, OFDM, and filtering.
* Biomedical Engineering: Analyzing electrical signals from the body like ECG (heart) and EEG (brain) traces to diagnose medical conditions.
* Control Systems: Managing aerospace autopilots, robotics, and industrial automation via feedback loops.
* Audio and Image Processing: Enhancing digital media, voice recognition software, and noise-canceling headphones.
