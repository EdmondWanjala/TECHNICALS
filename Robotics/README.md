                        ROBOTICS

Robotics is the interdisciplinary branch of engineering and science that designs, builds, and operates programmable machines to interact with the physical world.
    
    Overview of Robotics
A robot is an electromechanical system combining mechanical structures, sensors, actuators, power supplies, and a control system (the "brain").
* Sensors: Gather environmental data like light, temperature, or distance via LiDAR and cameras.
* Actuators: Act as muscles, converting power into physical motion or manipulation.
* Control Systems: Process data to make autonomous or semi-autonomous decisions.
    
    Key Topics in Robotics
* Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning: Gives robots real-time object recognition, adaptation, and autonomous navigation skills.
* Kinematics & Dynamics: Studies the motion and forces governing robot joints, arms, and locomotion paths without considering the forces that cause them.
* Soft Robotics: Uses flexible, compressible materials like silicone for safe handling of delicate tasks.
* Swarm Robotics: Coordinates many simple robots collectively to complete large-scale objectives.
* Human-Robot Interaction (HRI): Focuses on how robots safely and naturally cooperate alongside humans (such as collaborative cobots).
    
    Real-World Applications
* Manufacturing: Industrial robotic arms weld, paint, and assemble products on precise factory lines.
* Healthcare: Robotic systems assist surgeons with microscopic precision and transport hospital supplies.
* Agriculture: Autonomous tractors and weeders plant seeds, monitor health, and harvest crops with high precision.
* Logistics & Delivery: Autonomous mobile robots sort inventory in fulfillment hubs while delivery bots navigate streets.
* Exploration: Rovers like Perseverance explore hazardous or distant environments like Mars and deep ocean trenches.
